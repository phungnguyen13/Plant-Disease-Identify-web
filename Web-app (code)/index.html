About us

Introduction.

We are a platform that supports the identification of and information retrieval for crop diseases, helping users quickly determine the causes and effectively protect their harvests.

Purpose.

The goal of the system is to leverage image recognition technology and a scientific database to provide an accurate and user-friendly solution for diagnosing plant diseases.

Instruction.

You can enter the name of a disease into the search bar, or use the feature Camera to take a picture of diseased leaves, or to find details in the section Library. Press the button X to close this instruction panel.
